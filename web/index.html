InstaPunch Bridge

把 RTK、激光测距等经典蓝牙设备转为 BLE，供 InstaPunch Mapper 连接。
Turns classic-Bluetooth field devices into BLE for InstaPunch Mapper.

  1. 用 USB-C 数据线把 M5Stack Atom Lite 接到电脑。
    Connect the M5Stack Atom Lite over USB-C.
  2. 在桌面版 Chrome 或 Edge 中点击下方按钮，选择出现的串口。
    In desktop Chrome or Edge, click the button and pick the serial port.
  3. 烧录完成后，用 InstaPunch Mapper 连接 InstaPunch-Bridge-XXXX 并选择设备；也可在“Logs & Console”中输入 scan、pair 1 <MAC> [PIN]。
    After flashing, connect to InstaPunch-Bridge-XXXX from InstaPunch Mapper, or use scan / pair in “Logs & Console”.
